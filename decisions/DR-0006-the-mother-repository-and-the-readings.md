# DR-0006 · Delivery and Implementation mother: the mother repository, and the readings of 49GAION/DR-0041 and DR-0042

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION
- **Source:** the governing seat's word of 25 October 2026 (translated: "Yes.") on the proposal to move the three mothers of the design chain into repositories of 49GAION; `49GAION/DR-0024` (a mother repository for a Library part); `49GAION/DR-0041` (the complete definition) and `49GAION/DR-0042` (its clarifications), both approved by the governing seat on 25 October 2026.
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0005; it supersedes nothing and removes nothing. The blueprint stays `v0.2.1`, byte for byte.

## Decision

1. **This repository.** `github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-delivery-and-implementation`, public, is the mother repository of the Library part 49GAION Delivery and Implementation. It holds the mother blueprint, laid out and checked as a writing repository, for any organisation, with no organisation's data.
2. **Moved unchanged.** Every tracked file of the part's first-instance repository at its pinned commit `44d7dbf` is copied here as it was. The blueprint `blueprint/DI-BLUEPRINT.md` `v0.2.1` keeps its SHA-256 `b1b22ca74f0a6596fa7a7445d14beaa8fe91d0c50c7edaffa5cfaf6eb0295515`, the one the Library register pins. The decision records of the earlier versions come with it and stay as written. This record, the index row, the README head, the state and the changelog rows are the only additions.
3. **The Library pin moves here.** Once this text is merged, the Library register (`49GAION-protocol`, `library/README.md`) pins this part by this repository, the same path, the same version, the merged commit and the same SHA-256. The first instance's repository is that organisation's own and is not a text of the Library.
4. **The complete definition governs.** Where this repository differs from `49GAION-FRAMEWORK.md`, the definition governs, and this repository is read with it until its next version brings it into line. Nothing here is removed.
5. **A pattern of the Library.** This text is a pattern of the 49GAION Library. An organisation takes it, personalises it or builds something better, and what it takes is held in its own Library as its own rules. This part designs an organisation of human positions; how human and how much AI the organisation is, is the `[ORG]AION`'s own design work, prepared by it and decided by the governance seat (`49GAION/DR-0031`, DR-0037, DR-0041).
6. **The hub of the operation.** Every rule here that names `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, the Operating Core Office, or the human hub is read with the definition: the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, is the hub of the operation, both the human hub and the AI hub. Where this text places the part directly under the Charter, read: the part is an AIOC of the operation, in or under the Top Management Group, arranged by it through Structural Adaptation, where a human position decides each change (`49GAION/DR-0040`).
7. **Panel and back office.** The panel is the unit's interface, the organisation's own, written in code, from which every task of the unit can be controlled; the back office is the AI chat workspace where the unit's AI works, an authorised interface too and the keeper of the panel.
8. **The clarifications (DR-0042).** Where this text says that something always holds, it holds in every edition 49GAION hands over; after the handover it is the organisation's own, within the law. A fresh live sign-in is needed for each signature; a standing approval covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority. The Chief of Staff of every position here may order and summarise, never drop or delay; the raw record is always open to its position; a report, an objection or a stop never passes through any AI. Every Work Item carries its original requester, and an act runs only within the authority of both the unit that does it and the original requester. The Review Panel rule carried in this text (several engines from two or more families review each locked output; no engine judges its own work) stands; "one engine writes, two challenge" is the Library's default pattern, and this text keeps its own, stricter rule.
9. **Positions.** Every human seat here is a position, never a person. The governance seat appoints Top Management directly and keeps the final right over who holds any position; Top Management fills the positions below it.
10. **Powered by 49GAION.** An organisation that takes this part, or any other item of 49GAION, carries "Powered by 49GAION" on all its products.
11. **The version.** Unreleased. The blueprint stays at `v0.2.1`, its cards do not change. The readings are folded into the text at its next version, which reaches the Library only by a pull request that moves the pin, on the governing seat's word.
